# Home
SEI leads faculty professional development and curriculum innovation in environmental and sustainability education across the district, connecting teaching, campus operations, and community practice so environmental equity becomes integral to student learning.

Our work serves faculty, academic leaders, program directors, and institutional partners who want practical models for embedding sustainability across disciplines and campuses.

Through a statewide demonstration project, policy engagement, and partnerships that open campus facilities for learning, SEI turns colleges into living laboratories. Students benefit from internships that have expanded from 9 to more than 100 placements, linking coursework to applied sustainability roles. Community workshops and outreach extend this impact beyond campus.
