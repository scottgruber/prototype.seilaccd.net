# Professional Development
Faculty professional development is SEI’s core mission. We organize research, workshops, and hands-on learning that help educators embed environmental equity in their courses and programs.

The Demonstration Project offers a comprehensive model—integrating curriculum innovation, community practice, coaching, field trips, and culminating events—designed to show how sustainability can be woven into teaching at scale.

SEI supports faculty as they design new courses, from environmental politics to climate-focused humanities offerings, and as they integrate sustainability across disciplines. We help campuses build sustainability committees that bring faculty, staff, and administrators into shared governance, ensuring professional development is tied to institutional accountability.

Outcomes include faculty with practical tools, strengthened enrollment through relevant course offerings, and classrooms connected to the operations and policies that shape sustainable campuses.
