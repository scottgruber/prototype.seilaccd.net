# Resources
SEI provides practical materials that help faculty translate environmental equity into their teaching, including shared curriculum resources, training guides, and tools developed through professional development efforts.

The Demonstration Project supplies a replicable framework with curriculum outlines, community practice models, coaching approaches, field experiences, and culminating activities that other campuses can adapt.

Resources also support sustainability committees and partners who bridge facilities and instruction. Community-focused materials—such as workshops and guidance created for post-disaster recovery—extend SEI’s educational reach beyond campus.
