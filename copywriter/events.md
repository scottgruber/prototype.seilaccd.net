# Events
SEI convenes workshops, webinars, and trainings that address pressing climate topics such as heat, water, and fire, alongside sessions on sustainability practice for campuses and communities.

Field trips and culminating events within the Demonstration Project connect classroom learning to lived environments. SEI also represents and uplifts faculty work at conferences and statewide gatherings, sharing effective models for environmental equity in education.

Events engage faculty, staff, students, administrators, and community partners, advancing the broader mission of linking instruction, campus operations, and public resilience.
