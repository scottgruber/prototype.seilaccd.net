# About Us
SEI serves as the district’s in-house organizer and host for professional development in environmental and sustainability education, ensuring faculty have the support needed to integrate environmental equity into curriculum.

We bridge faculty, administration, facilities, and operations—turning campuses into living laboratories for sustainability and influencing how new construction and maintenance become part of teaching and research.

SEI advances policy and statewide leadership through collaborations with lobbyists, legislators, and initiatives such as Vision 2030, connecting local innovation to broader change. Partnerships include The Climate Center, Facilities teams, ASCCC, local government, UCLA, the Center for Diverse Leadership, and Indigenous communities, reflecting a commitment to shared environmental learning.

By representing faculty at conferences and statewide events, SEI amplifies campus achievements and positions the district as a leader in sustainability education. Our work is grounded in shared governance, community engagement, and the belief that clearly telling this story protects and sustains the initiative’s impact.
